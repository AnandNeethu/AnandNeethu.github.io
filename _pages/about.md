I’m a theoretical and computational chemist in the [Ultrafast Dynamics Laboratory](https://femto.postech.ac.kr/home) at Pohang University of Science and Technology (POSTECH). I study what happens to a molecule in the first femtoseconds after it absorbs light. I use quantum dynamics simulations alongside time-resolved spectroscopy to understand how nuclear motion drives transitions between electronic states.

## Research

My work on boron-dipyrromethene (BODIPY) dyes asks why closely related molecules relax at different rates. Using quantum wave-packet simulations, we traced those differences to the positions of conical intersections and to vibrations that act together. In hydroxychromones, I’ve studied how a transition between electronic states can drive proton transfer within a molecule.

I also develop graph neural networks that represent a light-absorbing molecule and its solvent separately. I use these models to predict properties such as emission wavelength, fluorescence quantum yield, and lifetime.
